# CoinNudge

> CoinNudge provides evidence-rich crypto market monitoring for individuals and research-ready derived market datasets for professional research workflows.

CoinNudge helps users notice and investigate meaningful market changes without watching every chart. It does not execute trades, custody funds, provide personalized financial advice, predict future prices or guarantee returns.

CoinNudge Data is a separate professional product line. It offers eligible CoinNudge-derived indicators, normalized event features and point-in-time research records through filtered API access, partitioned Parquet or CSV files, or a scoped one-time extract. It does not sell exchange-native payloads, full raw tick feeds, raw L2 books or private customer data.

## Data and methodology

- Binance Spot supplies the selected liquid USDT spot universe, candles, price, volume and executed-order-flow inputs.
- Coinbase public USD spot data supplements BTC and ETH observations; it is not described as measured deposits, withdrawals or total market capital flow.
- Hyperliquid supplies venue-specific perpetual funding and open-interest context.
- OKX supplies public forced-liquidation events used as venue-specific liquidation context.
- Binance and MEXC official announcements supply listing events.
- Technical alerts use closed candles. RSI, MACD, EMA and Bollinger conditions are interpreted with ADX/DMI trend strength, ATR volatility and UTC-day VWAP quality context.
- Missing, stale, conflicting or insufficient data is stated or filtered rather than presented as confirmation.
